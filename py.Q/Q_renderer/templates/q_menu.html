{"task": "[URL to CDex Task Resource]"}

For example:

{"task": "https://cdex-provider-server.davinci.hl7.org/fhir/Task/118"}

CDex's Requesting Attachments Using Questionnaires describes the technical workflow of the DTR app that this app is simulating. In a future version, this app will follow the SMART on FHIR launch and get the Questionnaires after it is launched
